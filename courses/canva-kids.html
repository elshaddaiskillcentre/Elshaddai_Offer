Canva Basics for Kids

A guided introduction to visual design using Canva’s free tools. Children learn layout, text, colours, images and design elements through age-appropriate projects.

Course Information

Duration15 guided sessions
Course fee₹1,500
Fee basisFixed course fee
Suitable forClasses 4–8
Batch dates, session timing and seat availability are confirmed by the centre before enrolment.

What you will learn

  • Choose suitable templates and page sizes
  • Arrange text, images and design elements clearly
  • Use colour and typography consistently
  • Export and present simple digital designs

Module-wise syllabus

Design basics

  • Canva workspace and template selection
  • Page size, layout and alignment
  • Text hierarchy, fonts and colour combinations

Visual elements

  • Shapes, icons, frames and image placement
  • Cropping, resizing and spacing
  • Readable design and visual balance

Project creation

  • Posters and event announcements
  • Greeting cards and simple presentations
  • Download, export and presentation

Practical activities & assessment

Guided explanation, hands-on practice and review. Activities are adjusted to the learner’s starting level and batch pace.

  • A class or event poster
  • A greeting card
  • A mini design portfolio

Eligibility & learning approach

For Classes 4–8. Basic computer use and access to a Canva account are required; setup guidance can be provided.

Training is delivered through guided sessions with opportunities to practise, ask questions and review completed work. The batch calendar is shared during admission counselling.
