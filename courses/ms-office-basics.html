MS Office Basics

A practical foundation in everyday office applications. Learners practise creating documents, organising basic data in spreadsheets and preparing clear presentations.

Duration30 guided sessions
Course fee₹5,000
Fee basisFixed offline course fee
Suitable forStudents and beginners
Batch dates, session timing and seat availability are confirmed before enrolment. For tuition and research support, charges are based on the hourly sessions taken.

What you will learn

  • Create and format basic Word documents
  • Enter data and use common Excel formulas
  • Prepare structured PowerPoint presentations
  • Save, organise and share office files

Module-wise syllabus

Computer and file skills

  • Desktop, folders and file organisation
  • Keyboard shortcuts and document handling
  • Saving, exporting and printing

Microsoft Word

  • Document creation and formatting
  • Page setup, tables, lists and images
  • Practical letters and reports

Excel and PowerPoint

  • Workbook, worksheet and cell basics
  • Simple formulas, functions and charts
  • Slide layouts, text, images and presentation delivery

Practical activities & assessment

Guided explanation, hands-on practice and review. Activities are adjusted to the learner’s starting level and batch pace.

  • Formatted document
  • Simple Excel workbook
  • Short PowerPoint presentation

Eligibility & learning approach

No previous office-software experience is required. Basic keyboard and mouse use is helpful.

Training is delivered through guided sessions with opportunities to practise, ask questions and review completed work. The batch calendar is shared during admission counselling.
