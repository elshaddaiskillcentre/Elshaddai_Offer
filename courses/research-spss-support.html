Academic Support

College Research & SPSS Tools Support

Hourly academic support for learners who need help understanding research-paper preparation steps or using SPSS tools. The exact work plan is agreed after reviewing the learner’s topic, data and requirements.

Suitable forCollege and research learners
Batch dates, session timing and seat availability are confirmed before enrolment. For tuition and research support, charges are based on the hourly sessions taken.

What you will learn

  • Understand stages of research-paper preparation
  • Learn the purpose of relevant SPSS procedures
  • Interpret outputs with appropriate academic caution
  • Organise analysis notes and presentation of findings

Module-wise syllabus

Research preparation

  • Topic, objectives and research questions
  • Literature organisation and basic structure
  • Planning data collection and variables

SPSS workflow

  • Data entry, coding and variable setup
  • Descriptive statistics and suitable procedures
  • Reading tables and interpreting outputs

Reporting support

  • Organising findings and tables
  • Explaining results in clear academic language
  • Reviewing limitations and next steps

Practical activities & assessment

Guided explanation, hands-on practice and review. Activities are adjusted to the learner’s starting level and batch pace.

  • Guided SPSS exercises using learner-approved data
  • Output interpretation practice
  • Research structure and presentation review

Eligibility & learning approach

Bring your research brief, questionnaire or dataset where applicable. Scope and feasibility are reviewed before the session.

Training is delivered through guided sessions with opportunities to practise, ask questions and review completed work. The batch calendar is shared during admission counselling.
