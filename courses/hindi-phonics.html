Hindi Phonics – Read & Write

A child-friendly introduction to Hindi sounds, letters, word formation and early reading, progressing to short sentences and guided writing.

Suitable forClasses 1–5
Batch dates, session timing and seat availability are confirmed by the centre before enrolment.

What you will learn

  • Recognise and pronounce Hindi sounds and letters
  • Connect sounds with letter forms and common matras
  • Read familiar words and short sentences
  • Write simple words and sentences through guided practice

Module-wise syllabus

Sound and letter foundations

  • Hindi vowels and consonants
  • Letter recognition, pronunciation and writing strokes

Word building

  • Common matras and sound combinations
  • Joining letters into simple words
  • Vocabulary through familiar themes

Reading and writing

  • Reading words and short sentences
  • Guided copying, dictation and spelling
  • Simple sentence writing and revision

Practical activities & assessment

Guided explanation, hands-on practice and review. Activities are adjusted to the learner’s starting level and batch pace.

  • Picture-to-word reading cards
  • Word-building and dictation exercises
  • A short reading and writing review

Eligibility & learning approach

For Classes 1–5. No previous Hindi reading experience is required.

Training is delivered through guided sessions with opportunities to practise, ask questions and review completed work. The batch calendar is shared during admission counselling.
