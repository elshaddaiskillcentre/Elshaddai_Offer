What you will learn

  • Understand chapter concepts and key terminology

Module-wise syllabus

Theory and concepts

  • Chapter-wise teaching based on the learner’s board syllabus
  • Definitions, explanations and short-answer preparation
  • Important long-answer structures and revision

Programming and practical work

  • Programming fundamentals and logic
  • Practical exercises aligned to the prescribed course
  • Debugging, output tracing and problem-solving

Exam preparation

  • One-mark and short-answer practice
  • Model questions and timed revision
  • Doubt-clearing and focused revision plans

Practical activities & assessment

Guided explanation, hands-on practice and review. Activities are adjusted to the learner’s starting level and batch pace.

  • Chapter-wise programming exercises
  • Short tests and model questions
  • Practical and theory revision

For Class 11 and Class 12 students following Tamil Nadu State Board or CBSE. Please specify subject and board when enquiring.

Training is delivered through guided sessions with opportunities to practise, ask questions and review completed work. The batch calendar is shared during admission counselling.
