MS Office for Professionals

A practical productivity course for learners who know basic computer operations and want to create polished office documents, use more capable spreadsheet tools and deliver clear presentations.

Course Information

Duration30 guided sessions
Course fee₹7,500
Fee basisFixed course fee
Suitable forCollege learners, job seekers and working professionals
Batch dates, session timing and seat availability are confirmed by the centre before enrolment.

What you will learn

  • Prepare professional Word reports and formatted documents
  • Use common advanced Excel tools for analysis and reporting
  • Create consistent, clear PowerPoint presentations
  • Organise work files and produce a small office portfolio

Module-wise syllabus

Professional Word

  • Styles, sections, headers and footers
  • Tables, images, page layout and document structure
  • Resume, letters and report formatting

Advanced Excel

  • Logical and lookup formulas at an introductory level
  • Sorting, filtering and data validation
  • Charts, summaries and PivotTable fundamentals

Professional PowerPoint

  • Slide design, hierarchy and consistency
  • Charts, images and visual storytelling
  • Presentation delivery and speaker notes

Workplace productivity

  • Templates and document organisation
  • Integrated reporting tasks
  • Portfolio review and practical assessment

Practical activities & assessment

Guided explanation, hands-on practice and review. Activities are adjusted to the learner’s starting level and batch pace.

  • Professional report or resume
  • Excel analysis and chart workbook
  • Business-style presentation

Eligibility & learning approach

Basic familiarity with Word, Excel and PowerPoint is recommended. Learners needing an introduction may start with MS Office Basics.

Training is delivered through guided sessions with opportunities to practise, ask questions and review completed work. The batch calendar is shared during admission counselling.
