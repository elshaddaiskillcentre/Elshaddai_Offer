Maths Basics – Syllabus Support

A foundational mathematics programme aligned to the learner’s current school level. The tutor identifies gaps and builds understanding through examples, visual methods and practice.

What you will learn

  • Improve number recognition and place value
  • Practise age-appropriate arithmetic operations
  • Apply concepts to simple word problems
  • Explain calculation steps with confidence

Module-wise syllabus

Number foundations

  • Numbers, number names and place value
  • Comparing, ordering and patterns
  • Skip counting and tables as appropriate

Core operations

  • Addition and subtraction strategies
  • Multiplication and division foundations
  • Mental maths and checking answers

Applied mathematics

  • Fractions and measurement at grade level
  • Time, money, shapes and data basics
  • Word problems from school topics

Practical activities & assessment

Guided explanation, hands-on practice and review. Activities are adjusted to the learner’s starting level and batch pace.

  • Class-level worksheets
  • Mental-maths drills
  • Concept review and progress check

Eligibility & learning approach

For Classes 1–5. Lessons are adapted to the child's current class and learning level.

Training is delivered through guided sessions with opportunities to practise, ask questions and review completed work. The batch calendar is shared during admission counselling.
